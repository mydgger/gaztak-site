A terrace in Agen, all year round

On rue Voltaire, under the string lights, GAZTAK’s terrace has 60 seats, open all year round. A covered area of 20 seats means you can enjoy it even on rainy evenings.

The terrace at a glance

60 seats

Right in the town centre

At 40 rue Voltaire, open Tuesday to Saturday from 6 pm, summer and winter.

20 covered seats

Even when it rains

Part of the terrace is covered. The terrace isn’t heated: in mid-winter, bring a jacket or choose the dining room (40 seats).

Dogs welcome

Outside and inside

Your dog is welcome to join you at GAZTAK.

What we serve on the terrace

The whole menu: farmhouse cheese and artisan charcuterie boards, the planchette for a nibble and, from October to April, all-you-can-eat raclette. To drink: easy-drinking wines, draught beers, prosecco on tap and cocktails, with or without alcohol.

Your terrace questions

Is the terrace open all year round?

Yes, Tuesday to Saturday from 6 pm, summer and winter.

Is the terrace heated?

No, but 20 seats are covered. On very cold nights, the dining room has 40 seats.

Can we book a table on the terrace?

Yes: book online and mention you’d like the terrace, or give us a call.

Are dogs allowed?

Yes, on the terrace and inside.

Can we book the terrace privately?

Yes, its 60 seats can also be booked privately. All about private hire
