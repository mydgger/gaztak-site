More than 20 people?

For more than around twenty people, GAZTAK can be booked privately: part of the venue for up to 40 standing guests, the whole venue for up to 70, or the 60-seat terrace. Group rates from 20 people, quotes on request.

All about private hire

Your group dining questions

How many people can come without private hire?

Up to around twenty. Beyond that, we offer private hire of part or all of the venue. All about private hire

Do we need to book for a group?

Yes, it’s strongly recommended: book online or call us and tell us how many you are.

Can we have raclette as a group?

Yes, from October to April: the traditional machine with its half wheel is served from 2 people and suits big tables very well. All about our all-you-can-eat raclette

Do you offer group rates?

From 20 people, we offer group rates, set case by case depending on your needs. We can prepare a quote on request.

Are there meat-free options?

Yes: cheese-only boards, raclette without the charcuterie, and we adapt our dishes as much as we can. All our cheese dishes

Planning a big table?

Book online and tell us how many you are, or give us a call to talk it through.
