All-you-can-eat raclette in Agen

All winter long, GAZTAK serves its famous all-you-can-eat raclette, just like in the mountains, right in the centre of Agen. Unlimited cheese, potatoes and condiments: a treat for raclette lovers from Agen and all over Lot-et-Garonne.

A raclette restaurant with a neighbourhood-bar feel

GAZTAK is first and foremost a cheese bar: raclette is served in the relaxed atmosphere of a true neighbourhood bar, with draught beers, easy-drinking wines and cocktails, with or without alcohol. The rest of the winter menu follows suit: Mont d’Or AOP baked with vin jaune, reblochonnade, beef and cheese skewers, and of course our farmhouse cheese and artisan charcuterie boards. Something for everyone, even those who aren’t raclette fans.

Where to eat raclette in Agen

At GAZTAK, 40 rue Voltaire, right in the centre of Agen. The Reine-Garonne and Mairie car parks are nearby, and the bar is wheelchair accessible. Whether you’re staying in Agen or visiting elsewhere in Lot-et-Garonne (Villeneuve-sur-Lot, Nérac, Marmande…), it’s the place for a raclette night.

Raclette is served from October to April, Tuesday to Saturday, from 6 pm.

Your raclette questions

Where can I eat raclette in Agen?

At GAZTAK, 40 rue Voltaire, in the town centre. All-you-can-eat raclette is served all winter, Tuesday to Saturday from 6 pm.

Which restaurant serves all-you-can-eat raclette in Agen?

GAZTAK, the cheese bar in Agen, serves all-you-can-eat raclette throughout the winter season, from October to April: unlimited cheese, potatoes and condiments, with a serving of charcuterie included.

Do I need to book for raclette?

It isn’t required, but it’s recommended: the option offered depends on the size of your group and that evening’s bookings.

Can I come on my own or as a couple?

Yes. Candle-heated raclette is served from 1 person; the traditional machine with its half wheel from 2 people.

Can we have a raclette night as a group?

Yes. The traditional machine with its half wheel is served from 2 people, and GAZTAK is available for private hire for birthdays or company evenings (40 seats inside, 60 on the terrace). Call us on +33 6 38 12 78 92 to talk it through.

Are there other melted-cheese dishes?

Yes: Mont d’Or AOP baked with vin jaune, reblochonnade, roasted camembert… See the full menu
