Your match night questions

Where can I watch the match in Agen?

At GAZTAK, 40 rue Voltaire, in the town centre: live sport on a 65-inch screen, Tuesday to Saturday from 6 pm.

Which matches do you show?

Live sport, rugby first: SU Agen, Top 14, the Six Nations… For a specific match, call us to check it’s on.

Do we need to book on match nights?

It’s recommended, especially for big matches and for groups.

Can we come as a group?

Yes, up to around twenty people without private hire. Coming as a group

Can we eat during the match?

Yes: boards, the planchette and, in winter, all-you-can-eat raclette or baked Mont d’Or. The kitchen usually stays open until 11 pm. See the full menu
