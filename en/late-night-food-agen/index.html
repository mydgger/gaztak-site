Late-night food in Agen

After a show, a match or a long day at work: at GAZTAK the kitchen usually stays open until 11 pm, and often later, Tuesday to Saturday, right in the centre of Agen. Drinks are served until midnight, or 1 am when it’s busy.

Our hours, with no nasty surprises

The kitchen

Usually until 11 pm

Boards, raclette and melted-cheese dishes usually served until 11 pm, and often later depending on how busy we are.

The bar

What you can eat late at GAZTAK

Farmhouse cheese and artisan charcuterie boards, the planchette for a nibble and, all winter, all-you-can-eat raclette, Mont d’Or AOP baked with vin jaune, reblochonnade or house-made beef and cheese skewers. A real dinner, even after 10 pm.

Your questions about our evening hours

How late can we eat at GAZTAK?

Usually until 11 pm, and often later depending on how busy we are.

How late can we have a drink?

Until midnight, and until 1 am when it’s busy.

Can we arrive after 10 pm?

Yes. To be sure of a table, book online or give us a call.

Can we have raclette late in the evening?

All-you-can-eat raclette is served from October to April, Tuesday to Saturday evenings. For a late raclette, mention it when you book. All about our all-you-can-eat raclette

Which days are you open?

Tuesday to Saturday, from 6 pm. Closed on Sunday and Monday.
