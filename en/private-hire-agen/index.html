Group rates and quotes

From 20 people, we offer group rates, set case by case depending on your needs and wishes. We can prepare a quote on request. A deposit confirms the booking; the amount depends on the overall budget.

How it works

  1. Call or email us with the date, number of guests and occasion.
  2. We talk through what you’d like: format, atmosphere and budget.
  3. We send you a quote if needed.
  4. You pay the deposit: the date is yours.

Your private hire questions

How many guests can you host?

Up to 70 standing guests if you book the whole venue, and 40 for part of it. For a seated meal, there are 40 seats inside and 60 on the terrace: we’ll work out what suits your event.

Can we book the terrace privately?

Yes. The 60-seat terrace, open all year round, can also be booked privately. Give us the date and number of guests and we’ll suggest the best option.

What are your group rates?

Group rates from 20 people, set case by case depending on your needs. Ask us for a quote.

Do you ask for a deposit?

Yes, to confirm the booking. The amount depends on the overall budget for the evening.

Can we have raclette as a group?

Yes, from October to April: the traditional machine with its half wheel is served from 2 people, perfect for a big table. All about our all-you-can-eat raclette

Where is GAZTAK?

At 40 rue Voltaire, right in the centre of Agen, close to the Reine-Garonne and Mairie car parks. The bar is wheelchair accessible.
