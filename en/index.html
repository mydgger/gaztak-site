A true neighbourhood bar where you eat well

Opened in 2022 on rue Voltaire, GAZTAK has become an Agen institution. People come for farmhouse cheeses, raw-milk whenever possible, from France and beyond, and artisan charcuterie served on sharing boards. At the bar: draught beers, easy-drinking wines and cocktails, with or without alcohol.

Listed in the Petit Futé guide since 2022

A 60-seat terrace, open all year round

Plus 40 seats inside.

All-you-can-eat raclette

All winter, just like in the mountains: unlimited cheese, potatoes and condiments, with a serving of charcuterie included.
