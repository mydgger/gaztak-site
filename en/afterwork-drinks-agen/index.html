Afterwork drinks in Agen

From 6 pm, Tuesday to Saturday, GAZTAK welcomes your afterworks and evening drinks right in the centre of Agen: a board to share, with a glass of wine, a draught beer or a cocktail, with or without alcohol.

By the glass and on draught

  • Wine by the glass: currently Chinon, Pinot Noir and Côtes du Rhône (the selection changes regularly)
  • Draught beers: light lager, IPA, Amsterdam wheat beer and Trappist amber ale
  • Prosecco on tap
  • Cocktails, with or without alcohol

Alcohol-free options are always on the menu.

A wine bar with a neighbourhood feel

No fuss here: easy-drinking wines by the glass, to go with farmhouse cheeses chosen according to what comes in. We serve until midnight, and until 1 am when it’s busy. Summer or winter, the 60-seat terrace is there for you, with 20 covered seats.

Your afterwork questions

What time can we come?

From 6 pm, Tuesday to Saturday. The bar is closed on Sunday and Monday.

Do we need to book?

It isn’t required, but it’s recommended, especially for a group.

Can we hold a team afterwork?

Yes: part of the bar can be booked for up to 40 standing guests, and the whole venue for up to 70. Group rates from 20 people. All about private hire

Do you have alcohol-free drinks?

Yes, alcohol-free cocktails are on the menu.

Can we eat after drinks?

Yes, the kitchen usually stays open until 11 pm: boards, all-you-can-eat raclette in winter, baked Mont d’Or… See the full menu
