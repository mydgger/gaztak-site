At GAZTAK, cheese is at the heart of everything: farmhouse cheese boards all year round and, in winter, all-you-can-eat raclette, baked Mont d’Or and reblochonnade. Right in the centre of Agen, on rue Voltaire.

Your questions about our cheese dishes

Where can I eat cheese in Agen?

At GAZTAK, the cheese bar in Agen, at 40 rue Voltaire. Farmhouse cheese boards all year round and melted-cheese dishes in winter, Tuesday to Saturday from 6 pm.

Which melted-cheese dishes do you serve?

From October to April: all-you-can-eat raclette, Mont d’Or AOP baked with vin jaune, reblochonnade and roasted camembert AOP. All about our all-you-can-eat raclette

Where do your cheeses come from?

They are farmhouse cheeses, raw-milk whenever possible, from France and beyond. The selection changes with what comes in.

Can I eat meat-free?

Yes: cheese-only boards, raclette without the charcuterie, and we adapt our dishes as much as we can. Mention it when you book.

Can we come as a group?

Yes, up to around twenty people without private hire. Coming as a group
